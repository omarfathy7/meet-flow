How do I reschedule a meeting? Meetings • Updated 2 days ago

To reschedule a meeting, navigate to the Meetings tab, select the meeting card, and click on the "Reschedule" button. Choose your new date and time and notify attendees automatically.

02
How does AI Summary work for meeting notes? Meetings • Updated 1 week ago

Our built-in AI listens to audio recordings, transcribes conversation points, extracts main decisions, and generates actionable summary notes within minutes after the call ends.

03
Can I connect MeetFlow to Google Calendar? Integrations • Updated 2 weeks ago

Yes! Go to the Integrations page in the sidebar, locate Google Calendar, and click "Connect". Follow the OAuth prompts to sync events instantly.

04
How do I upgrade or change my billing plan? Billing & Plans • Updated 3 weeks ago

Navigate to Settings > Billing, where you can view current plan details, upgrade to Pro/Enterprise tier, or switch your payment method easily.

05
How do I invite and manage team members? Team • Updated 2 weeks ago

Head to the Team section in your sidebar, click "Invite Member", type their email address, and assign an Admin or Member role.

06
Is my meeting data encrypted and secure? Account & Security • Updated 1 month ago

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 standards. We ensure strict SOC2 compliance and zero unauthorized access.
